Consulting

How I help revenue teams

From architecture and audits to hands-on builds shipped straight into production, on Salesforce or HubSpot.

01

CRM architecture: Salesforce and HubSpot

Salesforce: Apex (Queueable, Batch, Invocable), Flows, Custom Metadata Types, Named Credentials, and Pardot. Config-driven systems your admins can change without a deploy. HubSpot: pipeline and stage design, deal properties and scoring, forecasting, and migrations from legacy pipelines, built in a sandbox before any change reaches production.

02

AI automation

Claude and Gemini integrated into the revenue stack as infrastructure: case categorization, AI-written email, call coaching, and meeting intelligence. Rule-based checks handle the simple cases before the model runs.

03

Data pipelines and integrations

Reverse ETL between BigQuery and Salesforce, plus integrations with Slack, ChiliPiper, PandaDoc, and Dialpad. Every connector has service-account authentication, graceful degradation, and structured logging.

04

Production engineering

Node.js services on Google Cloud with systemd, cron, log rotation, and zero-downtime deployments. These are production systems with rollback plans and tests, not prototypes.
